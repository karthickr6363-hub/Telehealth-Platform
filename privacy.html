LEGAL FRAMEWORK

Protocol for Data Integrity

Last Updated: October 2026

1. Data Encryption Standards

Every byte of data transmitted via TeleHealth Pro is encrypted using military-grade AES-256 protocols. Your video consultations are peer-to-peer and never stored on persistent storage unless explicitly requested for medical record compliance.

2. HIPAA Compliance

We exceed the standards set by the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act. Our internal systems are audited quarterly by third-party security firms to ensure absolute patient confidentiality.

3. AI Ethics & Governance

Heal-GPT operates under strict ethical guidelines. No patient-identifiable data is used to train our large-scale models. All diagnostic suggestions are reviewed by human clinicians before being actioned in your wellness plan.
