Frequently Asked Questions

How do I book an appointment?

Simply sign up, choose a doctor, and select a time slot that works for you.

Is my data secure?

Yes, we use military-grade encryption to protect all your personal and medical information.

Are consultants available on weekends?

Yes, our platform host doctors who are available 24/7, including weekends and public holidays.

Do I get a prescription after the visit?

Yes, if the doctor deems it necessary, you will receive a digital e-prescription that can be used at any local pharmacy.
